In this folder, the data obtained with the PhytoPAM device by the participants of the PAM workshop at MOB are collected. Each subgroups (7 in total) obtained its own set of data exposing Chlamydomonas reinarhardii cells to varying light condition over time in order to measure, through fluorometry, their light adaptation dynamics and their photosynthetic efficiency. Cells were initially exposed to dark conditions and then to a given actinic light level. Additionally, actinic light were regularly switched off to measure F0' (fluoresence yield when all PSII centres are open) for a short 20s window. 
From, these data, photosynthetic effeciency and non-photochemical quenching of the cells were then calculated and plotted using the 'plot_NPQ_data' code provided in this repository.
